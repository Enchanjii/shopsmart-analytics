ABOUT SHOPSMART ANALYTICS

Retail decisions, grounded in data.

We are a fictional educational company concept exploring how machine learning can support retail analysis.

THE CHALLENGE

Retail data can be hard to turn into action.

Retailers make decisions about inventory, promotions, customer experience, and retention. Sales histories, feedback, and browsing activity can offer useful signals, but those signals need careful interpretation.

This classroom project demonstrates six machine learning approaches using reproducible datasets. The initial datasets will be synthetic—not real customer records.

RESPONSIBLE USE

Results need context.

  • Synthetic examples do not prove real-world performance.
  • Predictions show patterns, not causes or certainty.
  • Model metrics depend on the dataset and evaluation method.
  • People should review important decisions rather than rely on a score alone.
  • Customer data should be handled with privacy and fairness in mind.
MISSION

Accessible, data-driven insights

Help retail businesses make better decisions through accessible, data-driven insights.

VISION

Practical machine learning

Become a trusted provider of practical and understandable machine learning solutions for modern retailers.

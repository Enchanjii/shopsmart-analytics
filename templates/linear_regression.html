MACHINE LEARNING LAB · MODEL 01

Linear Regression: sales forecasting

Estimate sales amount from retail activity. The data and model are synthetic educational examples, not production evidence.

BUSINESS QUESTION

How might sales change with store conditions?

Linear Regression estimates a continuous value by fitting a linearly weighted combination of the input features. Retail teams could use such a forecast as one input to sales and inventory planning.

Why this model? Sales amount is a numeric target, and a linear model is a clear baseline whose feature relationships can be inspected and explained.

Why this data? Advertising, price, discounts, traffic, prior sales, and calendar indicators are plausible planning signals a retailer could collect. The generated sales target makes this a supervised regression task, but the formula is simplified and does not establish real business relationships.

Limitations: The generated data follows a simplified formula. Real sales can involve nonlinear patterns, changing customer behavior, external events, and measurement errors. A feature relationship is not proof of cause.
